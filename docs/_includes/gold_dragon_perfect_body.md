**Gold Dragon**

You gain immunity to poison, disease, fear, confusion, paralysis and sleep, and energy resistance equal to your character level + half your mythic rank. Saving throw bonuses below 5 + mythic rank and attributes below 14 are increased to those thresholds. Your highest ability score gains +4.

Your hit dice become d12s. Damage spells use at least d6s; spells already using d6s or larger instead deal +1 damage per die rolled. All your weapons count as +5 weapons.
