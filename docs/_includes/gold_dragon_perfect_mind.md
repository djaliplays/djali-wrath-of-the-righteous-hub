**Gold Dragon**

You gain immunity to mind-affecting effects and spell resistance equal to 15 + your character level + half your mythic rank. You can convert all damage you deal to holy damage, or unholy damage if you have vowed to serve Dahak.

Your base attack bonus is increased to 15 + mythic rank if lower. All skills become class skills, and skill ranks below your mythic rank are increased to that value. Attributes below 18 are increased to 18. Your highest ability score bonus increases to +6.

Damage spells use at least d8s; spells already using d8s or larger instead deal +1 damage per die rolled.
