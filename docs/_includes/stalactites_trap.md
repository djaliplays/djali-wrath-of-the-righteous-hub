**Area Trap**

- **Spell level:** Half your character level (maximum 9)
- **School:** Conjuration
- **Action:** Free action
- **Range:** Close
- **Target:** All enemies within a 5-foot burst
- **Duration:** 1 hour/level (how long the trap stays on the ground)
- **Saving throw:** Reflex half
- **Spell resistance:** Not affected
- **Save DC:** Varies; shown on the ability

Create Stalactites trap.

Granted by *Cave Fangs: Stalactites*, which also holds the trap's effect text. In game the ability is titled "Cave Fangs".

**Source:** [Pathfinder: Kingmaker Wiki](https://pathfinderkingmaker.fandom.com/wiki/Cave_Fangs_(Stalactites))
