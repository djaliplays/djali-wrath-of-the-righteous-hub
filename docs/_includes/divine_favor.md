**Personal Spell**

- **Level:** 1
- **School:** Evocation 
- **Duration:** 1 minute

Calling upon the strength and wisdom of a deity, you gain a +1 luck bonus on attack and weapon damage rolls for every three caster levels you have (at least +1, maximum +3). The damage bonus doesn't apply to spell damage.

**Source:** [Pathfinder: Kingmaker Wiki](https://pathfinderkingmaker.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_Favor)
