**Azata Mythic Spell, 6th Level**

- **Target:** All allies within a 60-foot burst, centred on the caster
- **Duration:** 1 round per 2 caster levels
- **Action:** Standard action

The Azata summons the power of Life itself. The Azata's and their companions' attacks deal Holy damage for the duration.

The spell also restores all companions' spell slots up to 5th level. It does not restore the Azata's mythic spellbook.
