**Lich Power**

For up to 3 rounds per day, the Lich can imbue all spells they cast with eclipse chill. Creatures affected by these spells must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw (DC = 10 + half the Lich's character level + their mythic rank + their highest ability modifier) or become blinded and take 2d8 + mythic rank cold damage. They also become vulnerable to cold and negative energy until the end of combat.
