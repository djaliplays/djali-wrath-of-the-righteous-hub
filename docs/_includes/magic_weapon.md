**Level:** 1
**School:** Transmutation
**Target:** Friendly within touch range
**Duration:** 1 minute/level

Magic weapon gives a weapon a +1 enhancement bonus on attack and damage rolls. An enhancement bonus doesn't stack with a masterwork weapon's +1 bonus on attack rolls.

**Source:** [Pathfinder: Kingmaker Wiki](https://pathfinderkingmaker.fandom.com/wiki/Magic_Weapon)
