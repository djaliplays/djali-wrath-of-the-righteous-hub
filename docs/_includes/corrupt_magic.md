**Lich Mythic Spell**

- **Level:** 9
- **School:** Abjuration
- **Target:** One creature
- **Range:** Medium
- **Saving Throw:** —
- **Casting Time:** Standard Action

This functions as a targeted dispel magic, but it can dispel multiple spells, starting with the highest-level spells and proceeding to lower-level spells. Additionally, the target receives a –1 penalty on attack and damage rolls, saving throws, and to AC for every dispelled effect.
