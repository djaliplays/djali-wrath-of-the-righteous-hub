**Minor Demon Aspect**

The Demon adopts the aspect of the incubus, gaining a bonus on all Persuasion, Athletics, and Lore (nature) skill checks equal to half the Demon's mythic rank plus 1. Those bonuses don't stack with other bonuses from demonic aspects. 

Whenever the Demon with the aspect of the incubus casts a spell or uses an ability during a demonic rage, its DC increases by 1. This bonus increases by 1 at 6th and 9th mythic ranks. 

While in a demonic rage, the Demon with the aspect of the incubus gains a +4 bonus on weapon damage rolls. The bonus to damage increases by 2 at 6th and 9th mythic ranks.
