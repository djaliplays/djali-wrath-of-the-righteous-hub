**Robe**

If the wearer of this robe is a spellcaster, their caster level is increased by 3, the save DC against all their spells is increased by 1, and they gain a +5 competence bonus on caster level checks to overcome spell resistance.

**Acquisition**

Dropped by the boss Demon-May-Care Joestantin on Treasure Island (Treasures of the Midnight Isles DLC, Act 4.3).
