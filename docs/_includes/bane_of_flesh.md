**Ring**
Once per day, whenever your HP falls below 0, your HP is restored by twice your character level, and the ring summons 1d4+2 undead skeletal champions around you for 2 minutes.

!!! bug "Fire Conversion"
    When the ring's once-per-day effect triggers upon the wielder falling below 0 HP, it also applies an unintended buff that grants +2 to attack rolls and converts elemental spell damage to Fire.

    It is removed upon rest.

**Acquisition**
Relic Broken Phylactery of Stevanius the Rotten
Crusade Battle south from Gargoyle Canyon (Act 2)
