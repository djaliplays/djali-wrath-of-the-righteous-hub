**Titan Fighter**

At 1st level, a titan fighter learns to wield a two-handed melee weapon in one hand (except double weapons). It is treated as one-handed when determining the effects of the Power Attack feat, adding the Strength bonus on damage rolls, and for all other purposes. All special properties of the weapon are preserved. The titan fighter takes an additional -2 penalty on attack rolls when using a two-handed weapon in one hand.
