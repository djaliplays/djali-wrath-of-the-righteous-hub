**Creature Trait**

You take no damage from non-magic weapons, you also take only half damage from any source which deals neither ghost, holy, divine or force damage.

!!! note "Additional Effects"
    The description omits a couple.
    
    - Immunity to physical damage from spells.
    - Immunity to precision damage, including Sneak Attack.
    - Immunity to critical hits.
    - Immunity to trip attempts.
    - Gains the Airborne trait.
    - Deflection bonus to AC equal to the Charisma modifier.
    - Uses Dexterity instead of Strength for melee attack rolls and combat manoeuvres.
