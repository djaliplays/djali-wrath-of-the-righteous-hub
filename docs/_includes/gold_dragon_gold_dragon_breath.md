**Gold Dragon**

You can breathe holy energy in a 50-foot cone, dealing (character level + half mythic rank)d8 holy damage. A successful Reflex save (DC = 10 + half character level + mythic rank + your highest ability modifier) halves the damage.

Enemies struck take a –1 penalty on all d20 rolls; enemies that fail the save instead take a –2 penalty. You can use this ability once every 1d4 rounds. The cooldown decreases to 1d2 rounds at mythic rank 9 and to 1 round at rank 10.
