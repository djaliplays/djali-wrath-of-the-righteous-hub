**Trickster Spell**

- **Level:** 5 
- **School:** Conjuration
- **Casting Time:** Standard Action
- **Spell Resistance**: Not affected
- **Target:**  All creatures within a 20 foot burst, centered on target point within medium range.

**In-Game Description**

You create a rain of halberds, making an attack roll against every creature in a 10-foot radius. A successful attack deals 1d10 points of slashing and piercing damage per caster level, and can critically strike.

**Effect**

Deals 1d10 slashing and piercing damage per caster level to all creatures within a 20 foot area.
