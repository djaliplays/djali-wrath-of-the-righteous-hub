"Demon Mythic Spell"

- **Level:** 3
- **School:** Transmutation
- **Duration:** Rounds/CL
- **Target:** All enemies within a 40 foot burst, centered on caster.
- **Saving Throw:** —
- **Casting Time:** Standard Action

Every enemy in a 40-foot radius receives a –2 penalty on attack and damage rolls, caster level checks to overcome spell resistance, concentration checks, and all saving throws.
