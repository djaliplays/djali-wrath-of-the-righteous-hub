**Quarterstaff +3**

This is a +3 quarterstaff. Whenever you cast the same spell three times in a row, your next spell becomes empowered, as though using the Empower Spell feat, and maximized, as though using the Maximize Spell feat.

**Acquisition**

Made from the relic Wicked Dope, found in the Crusade battle southwest of the Lands of Yath Watch (Act 3).
