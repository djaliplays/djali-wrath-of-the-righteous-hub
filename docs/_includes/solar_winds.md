**Angel**

Whenever Angelic Halo is active, all allies in a 50-foot area get a bonus to fire, cold, acid, and electricity resistances equal to 5 per the Angel's mythic rank, and a bonus to sonic and negative energy resistance equal to 3 per the Angel's mythic rank, as well as a bonus to force, unholy, holy, and divine resistance equal to the Angel's mythic rank.
