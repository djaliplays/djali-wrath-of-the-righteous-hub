- **Level:** 3
- **School:** Necromancy
- **Target:** Self
- **Duration:** 1 round/level

You know how to gain strength from fallen allies and enemies alike. You gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls, saving throws, and damage rolls for every dead creature in a 30-foot radius up to a maximum of +3 plus 1 per five caster levels. If the bonus is +3 or higher, you additionally gain the effects of the haste spell.
