**Lich Power**

The Lich gains a +4 profane bonus on attack rolls and a +1 profane bonus per mythic rank on damage rolls against frightened creatures, or ones under any mind-affecting effects.

Additionally, all enemies within 10 feet of the Lich must make a Will saving throw (DC = 10 + half the Lich's character level + their mythic rank + their highest ability modifier) or become shaken until the end of combat. A successful save gives immunity to this ability for 24 hours.
