**Personal Spell**

- **Level:** 5
- **School:** Conjuration
- **Casting time:** Standard action
- **Duration:** 10 minutes/level
- **Saving throw:** Reflex half
- **Spell resistance:** Not affected

Creates a magical trap that makes stalactites or stalagmites "bite" enemies moving through the affected area. You choose the variant when you cast the spell. For the duration you can place traps anywhere within close range as a free action (a swift action for Stalagmites). Each trap has a 5-foot radius and shortens the spell's remaining duration by 10 minutes.

**Variants:** Cave Fangs: Stalactites, Cave Fangs: Stalagmites

**Source:** [Pathfinder: Kingmaker Wiki](https://pathfinderkingmaker.fandom.com/wiki/Cave_Fangs)
