- **Level:** 6 or 5 (Bard)
- **School:** Abjuration
- **Target:** One creature
- **Range:** Medium
- **Saving Throw:** —
- **Casting Time:** Standard Action

This functions as dispel magic, except that it affects everything within a 20-foot-radius burst or it can dispel multiple spells, starting with the spell with the highest caster level and proceeding to spells with lower caster level.
