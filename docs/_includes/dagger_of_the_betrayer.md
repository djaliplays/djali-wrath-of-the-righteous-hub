**Dagger +5**

This +5 cruel anarchic dagger allows its wielder to channel magic through it, making it more powerful. The save DCs of all spells the wielder casts increase by 2. If the wielder is an arcanist with the magic deceiver archetype, the save DCs of fused spells they cast increase by 2 in addition to the first bonus.

**Acquisition**

Sold by the Shopkeeper (Equipment) in the A Dance of Masks DLC (Act 5).
