**Glasses**

The mask of Razmir himself. This mask grants its wearer immunity to spells from the Divination school, a +8 enhancement bonus to all mental ability scores, and the spell turning ability. The first spell the wearer casts in a round is cast as a free action.

!!! bug "Razmir's Spell Reflection"
    The mask reflects more than spells. It can also reflect abilities such as Vital Strike and Charge. Normal Spell Reflection only affects spells subject to spell resistance.
