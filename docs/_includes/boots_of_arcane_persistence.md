**Boots**

Whenever the wearer casts the same spell for the fourth time during the day, their next spell is quickened, as though using the Quicken Spell feat.

**Quicken Spell:** Casting a quickened spell is a swift action. You can perform another action, including casting another spell, in the same round. A spell with a casting time of more than 1 round or 1 full-round action cannot be quickened.

*Acquisition*: Sold by Krebus in Middle City (Act 4).
