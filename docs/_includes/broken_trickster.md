**Glasses**

This mask grants its wearer immunity to poison and a +6 enhancement bonus to Wisdom and Charisma ability scores. It also makes it impossible to identify the wearer's alignment.

Whenever the wearer takes damage of any energy type, they gain resistance 20 to this energy for 1 round. Whenever the wearer takes physical damage, they gain DR 20/— for 1 round.

**Acquisition**

Found in Laughter's Prison (Midnight Isles DLC, Act 3).
