**Gold Dragon**

You gain immunity to mind-affecting effects and spell resistance equal to 15 + your character level + half your mythic rank. You can change all damage you deal to holy damage (or unholy damage if you have vowed to serve Dahak). If your base attack bonus is less than 15 + mythic rank, you gain a bonus up to that number. All skills become class skills for you. If you have less ranks in a skill than your mythic rank, you gain a bonus up to that number. If any of your attributes before modifiers is less than 18, you gain a bonus up to that number. The bonus to your highest ability score increases to +6. Any spells dealing damage now change their dice to d8, if it was not d8 or greater. If it already was d8 or greater, instead the spell deals 1 additional point of damage per dice rolled.
