**Lich Power**

The Lich gains a +4 profane bonus on attack rolls and a profane bonus on damage rolls equal to their mythic rank against frightened creatures and creatures affected by mind-affecting effects.

Additionally, enemies within 10 feet must succeed at a Will saving throw (DC = 10 + half the Lich's character level + their mythic rank + their highest ability modifier) or become shaken until the end of combat. A successful save grants immunity to this ability for 24 hours.
