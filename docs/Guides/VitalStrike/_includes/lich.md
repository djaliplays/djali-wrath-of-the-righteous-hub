### Bypassing Sneak Immunity

??? lich-feature "Death of Elements"
    {% include '_includes/lich_death_of_elements.md' %}

Although the description specifies elemental damage, the ability appears to convert all the Lich's damage, including precision (sneak attack) damage dealt by weapons.

This conversion alone does not bypass immunity to precision damage. However, combining it with Ascendant Element: Negative Energy does.
