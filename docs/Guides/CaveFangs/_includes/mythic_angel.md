### Angel { #mythic-angel }

#### Mythic 3

At Mythic Rank 3, the Angel unlocks Sword of Heaven, which gives two extra damage dice to spells and abilities. This affects both the traps and Geomancy.

Its duration is very limited at this stage, with only two uses per rest. This allows for its use in two fights per rest.

#### Mythic 4

At Mythic Rank 4, the Angel gains access to the first Improved Sword of Heaven upgrade.

Everlasting Flame increases Sword of Heaven's duration to one minute per Mythic Rank. This brings it to three uses per rest, each lasting four minutes.

??? angel-feature "Everlasting Flame"
    {% include '_includes/everlasting_flame.md' %}

#### Mythic 6 { #angel-m6 }

At Mythic Rank 6, the Angel unlocks Sword of Holy Damage, which converts all damage to Holy while Sword of Heaven is active.

Sword of Heaven now has four uses per rest, each lasting six minutes. This is generally sufficient to maintain the effect throughout a dungeon and its encounters.

Around this point, the Storyteller quest also allows the restoration of the Ancient Cloak, rewarding The Bound of Possibility, which increases Sword of Heaven's damage dice by another two.

#### Mythic 7+

Further Improved Sword of Heaven upgrades become available at higher Mythic Ranks.


#### Discussion

The Angel has excellent damage potential at Mythic Rank 6. However, Sword of Heaven has a short duration at earlier ranks, and the lack of damage conversion before Mythic Rank 6 can make incorporeal enemies problematic.

A potential approach is to combine Cave Fangs with a conventional Bolt of Justice Angel build.

??? spell "Bolt of Justice"
    {% include '_includes/bolt_of_justice.md' %}

Bolt of Justice or Storm can handle problematic enemies, while traps deal with the rest. Both easily fit into the same build.

Plague of Madness can also prepare Bolt of Justice, so Bolster is the only essential metamagic feat at the start. (Promise of Greatness, which provides the Bolster effect, is not available until Act 4.)

Once Cave Fangs reaches its full potential, Bolt of Justice can either remain the primary standard-action option, or be replaced by another ability, such as Hymn, a high caster-level Greater Dispel or even a Hex (if conserving resources).

A Wildland Shaman is my prefered class. It can start as a melee-oriented build and then transition into a blaster. Cave Fangs is already on its spell list, and the class provides an animal companion, hexes, and unexpensive access to metamagic feats through the Secret hex choice. Though Flame Curse is not available to this archetype.

??? hex "Secret"
    {% include '_includes/shaman_secret.md' %}

As a Wisdom-based caster, the Shaman has lower potential trap DC than an equivalent Charisma- or Intelligence-based build. However, Bolt of Justice can help address enemies with high Reflex saves.

A two-level dip into Sacred Huntmaster can also be useful. Cunning Initiative adds Wisdom to initiative checks, while the Luck domain provides Bit of Luck. Both abilities help improve initiative reliability.

I would not recommend Hell Knight as damage is already high; flexibility, initiative and mobility are more important.
