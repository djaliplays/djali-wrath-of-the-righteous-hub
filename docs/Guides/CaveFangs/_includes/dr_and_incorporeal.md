### Damage Reduction

Physical spells have a pretty big downside in WotR: they are affected by DR.

Weapon damage has a huge number of ways to bypass (various levels of) DR. 

For energy spell damage, Ascendant Element and the Vulnerability Hex bypass energy resistances and immunities, see [Resistances and immunities](#resistances-and-immunities).

I only know of three ways to bypass DR for physical damage spells:

- [Smite (and Mark of Justice)](#smites)
- Conversion to energy, see [Holy Conversion](#holy-conversion) and [Negative Energy Conversion](#negative-energy-conversion)
- The [Demon's Aspect of Deskari](#demon-m9)


#### How much DR matters

DR matters a lot if you only have the base spell. It deals 3d8, so 3 to 24 damage, but 90% of the time it falls between 7 and 20. That is halved on a successful Reflex save.

Even regular late Act 2 enemies like Vrocks have DR 10, which means that on a successful save all the damage is likely to be mitigated.

Once you have the traps [maximized and empowered](#meta-plague), you deal 36 damage every cast without any further buffs, although those further buffs are hard to come by. Enemies with more than 15 DR are pretty rare. It is a significant damage nerf, but it does not invalidate the ability, and you can bring a Paladin with [Mark of Justice](#mark-of-justice) for select enemies.

### Incorporeal

Incorporeal is a much bigger problem.

    {% include '_includes/trait_incorporeal.md' %}

While the text says weapons, it does in fact include physical damage spells. Incorporeal enemies, for example Shadow Demons, which are relatively common from late Act 3 onwards, are completely immune to the damage of your traps.

I only know of two ways to deal with this:

- The Demon's [Aspect of Deskari](#demon-deskari), which is a mythic rank 9 ability, so just before the end of the game.
- Conversion to energy: the earliest is mythic rank 3 for the [Lich](#lich-m3), then mythic rank 6 for the [Angel](#angel-m6).

Note that converting to negative energy still means incorporeal enemies take only half damage. For holy damage it is full damage.

Incorporeal also gives immunity to Sneak Attacks which matter for [Surprise Spells](#surprise-spells). It can be bypassed trough holy conversion.
