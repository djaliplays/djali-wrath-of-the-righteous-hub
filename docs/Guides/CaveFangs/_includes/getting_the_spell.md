## Getting the Spell

### Spellbooks

Cave Fangs is a 5th-level spell in the spellbooks listed. The class level column indicates the earliest class level at which 5th-level spells become available.

| Class | Spell level | Class level |
|---|---|---|
| Druid | 5 | 9 |
| Shaman | 5 | 9 |
| Witch | 5 | 9 |
| Stigmatized Witch (spontaneous) | 5 | 10 |
| Oracle (Nature mystery, spontaneous) | 5 | 10 |
| Hunter | 5 | 13 |

### Loremaster

Any 9-level or 6-level caster can also get Cave Fangs through a Loremaster dip, by taking the *Druid Spell* option of the Secret class feature. When that becomes available depends on the caster type, so it is not listed here.
