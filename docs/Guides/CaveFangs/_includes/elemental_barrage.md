#### Elemental Barrage

??? mythic-feature "Elemental Barrage"
    {% include '_includes/elemental_barrage.md' %}

Although the description refers to energy damage, the ability requires elemental damage to function. Two different elements are therefore needed. Fire is available through the Geomancer dip and its bloodline; electricity is the easiest second element to obtain.

??? item "Stormlord's Resolve"
    {% include '_includes/stormlords_resolve.md' %}

Despite its description, Stormlord's Resolve does not convert all spell damage to electricity. It affects only energy damage, so energy conversion is required before it can work with Cave Fangs.

Toggling between fire and electricity conversion between casts causes affected enemies to take an additional 1d6 damage per mythic rank.

The main drawback is the mythic feat cost. The following selections are required:

- Elemental Barrage
- Ascendant Element: Fire
- Ascendant Element: Electricity

Vulnerability Curse can replace the latter two selections. See [Resistances and immunities](#resistances-and-immunities).
