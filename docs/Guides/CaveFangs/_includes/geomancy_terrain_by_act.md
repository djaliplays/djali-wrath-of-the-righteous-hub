##### Act 3 { #geomancy-terrain-act-3 }

The Act 3 areas are listed below by terrain. "Unknown" is the terrain the game itself reports in some places, and Geomancy generally does not work there.

| Terrain | Areas | |
|---|---|---|
| **Desert** | Baphomet's Shrine<br>Core of the Riddle (outside)<br>Desolate Hovel<br>Extirpator's Camp<br>Heart of Mystery (outside) | Ivory Sanctum (outside)<br>Place of Execution (outside)<br>Ravaged Long House (outside)<br>Temple of the Good Hunt (outside)<br>Terendelev's Lair |
| **Underground** | Areelu's Lab<br>Blackwater<br>Core of the Riddle<br>Dragon Burial Ground<br>Heart of Mystery<br>Ivory Sanctum | Legacy of the Ancients<br>Molten Scar<br>Place of Execution<br>Pulura's Fall<br>Ravaged Long House<br>Wintersun (both caves) |
| **Urban** | Artisan's Tower (Greenhouse) | Greengates |
| **Forest** | Ashen Grotto<br>Grimwood<br>Ruins of Ashberry Hamlet<br>Sacred Lands | Scrubland by a Bend in the River<br>Shrine of Sacrilege (outside)<br>Wintersun |
| **Highlands** | Artisan's Tower<br>Hellknights Outpost | Legacy of the Ancients (outside) |
| **Unknown** | Crimson Dust<br>Shrine of Sacrilege (inside) | Temple of the Good Hunt (inside) |

!!! note "Unknown terrain in the Temple of the Good Hunt"
    One of the Unknown areas, the inside of the Temple of the Good Hunt, did trigger Desert Geomancy.

##### DLC 3 (Treasure of the Midnight Isles)

The Treasure of the Midnight Isles main campaign dungeons can be visited in Act 3, 4 and 5.
The terrain type of all is Abyss.
