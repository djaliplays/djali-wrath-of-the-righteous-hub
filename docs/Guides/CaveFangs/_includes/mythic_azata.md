### Azata { #mythic-azata }

#### Mythic Ranks 4 and 6 { #azata-m4 }

The Azata gains a Superpower at Mythic Ranks 4, 6, 8 and 10. Two Superpowers have particular synergy with Cave Fangs.

Favorable Magic makes enemies less likely to succeed on saving throws. Even when they succeed, the ability increases the damage still dealt.

??? azata-feature "Favorable Magic"
    {% include '_includes/azata_favorable_magic.md' %}

Incredible Might adds damage equal to the Azata's Mythic Rank as a morale bonus. This applies to both Stalactite Traps and Geomancy.

??? azata-feature "Incredible Might" 
    {% include '_includes/azata_incredible_might.md' %}

!!! bug "Ring of Triumphant Advance"
    The Ring of Triumphant Advance does not apply its bonus to non-ray spells.

??? item "Ring of Triumphant Advance"
    {% include '_includes/ring_of_triumphant_advance.md' %}

As a result, the ring does not double the morale damage from Incredible Might.

#### Mythic Rank 8

The Azata gains access to the 6th-level mythic spell Joy of Life at Mythic Rank 8.

??? spell "Joy of Life"
    {% include '_includes/joy_of_life.md' %}

Joy of Life converts the attacks of the Azata and nearby allies to Holy damage. Its duration is only 1 round per 2 caster levels.

#### Mythic Rank 8: Devil { #devil-m8 }

The Azata can transition into the Devil path at Mythic Rank 8. All significant features are retained, with the main trade-off being the loss of the final three Azata levels. The most notable loss is Joy of Life, which would otherwise become available at Mythic Rank 8.

This interaction applies to both the Cave Fangs buff and the Stalactite Trap ability, potentially providing up to **+10 DC** at Mythic Rank 10.

#### Discussion

The main weakness of the Azata is the lack of damage conversion at Mythic Rank 3. Staying on the Azata path provides no conversion until Mythic Rank 8, and Joy of Life only offers a very short duration per cast. 

Incredible Might provides relatively little additional damage without the Ring of Triumphant Advance interaction.
