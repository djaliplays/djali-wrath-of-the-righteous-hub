### Lich { #mythic-lich }

#### Mythic 3 { #lich-m3 }

At Mythic Rank 3, the Lich can select its first power. For a dedicated Cave Fangs build, the strongest choice is Death of Elements.

??? lich-feature "Death of Elements"
    {% include '_includes/lich_death_of_elements.md' %}

Allowing for immediate geomancy use and bypassing of DR. See [Negative Energy Conversion](#negative-energy-conversion).

Another interesting option is Withering Life.

??? lich-feature "Withering Life"
    {% include '_includes/lich_withering_life.md' %}

This is more relevant to a Primal Regression Lich, where it can act as a free-action enabler, rather than to a dedicated Cave Fangs build. You would still want another option available, for example Stone Call with a Lesser Quicken Rod, against enemies with high Reflex saves.

#### Mythic 5

The Lich gains no specific benefits for Cave Fangs at this rank. However, the [Gloves of Enduring Wizard](#temporary-hit-points) can be found in Act 4, allowing you to take Ascendant Element: Negative without risking self-damage from Geomancy.

#### Mythic 6+

The Lich gains additional powers at Mythic Ranks 6, 8 and 10. None are particularly strong for Cave Fangs.

Fear Control can potentially add some damage to both traps and Geomancy.

??? lich-feature "Fear Control"
    {% include '_includes/lich_fear_control.md' %}

Eclipse Chill only affects Geomancy directly, but its effects can also benefit trap damage. However, it relies on a saving throw that enemies are likely to pass.

#### Discussion 

The Lich is arguably the best choice for an early, dedicated Cave Fangs build because damage conversion becomes available early. 

The merged spellbook also makes the Lich a natural fit for a full Arcane caster. Since the path has relatively few options for increasing Cave Fangs damage directly, an Arcane Trickster build is an obvious choice. Surprise Spells provides the missing additional damage.

This makes the Lich more specialised around Cave Fangs than the suggested Angel build, which retains Bolt of Justice as an alternative damage option.

The good old solo cheese build: Rowdy Rogue Witch of the Veil, is an obvious candidate, but the playstyle can become extremely tedious. Low BAB and the Witch's limited self-buffing options can make fights, particularly in Act 2, lengthy.

Vivisectionist is a better source of the initial sneak attack dice to meet the Arcane Trickster prerequisite if going for highest cap; see the [Gold Dragon](#mythic-gd)

An Eldritch Scoundrel is another an option I'm exploring. However, its six-level spell progression means an unmerged spellbook and it's got anti-synergy with Arcane Trickster due to it as well.
