### Metamagic

Metamagic can be applied to Cave Fangs normally, either by taking the relevant feat and adding it to the spellbook, or by using metamagic rods. Quicken and Extend rods can be found throughout the game. The available options are Quicken, Extend, Heighten and Completely Normal (Trickster only).

Only Heighten is useful.

#### Heighten

Heighten increases the DC of the Cave Fangs buff, and the traps inherit that DC. See [DC](#dc).

#### Quicken

Quicken would technically allow three Cave Fangs buffs in one fight: one cast before combat, one as a standard action, and one quickened. A Demon or Aeon could increase this to four. However, more than one buff is never necessary.

##### Plague of Madness { #meta-plague }

??? item-weapon "Plague of Madness"
    {% include '_includes/plague_of_madness.md' %}

Cast a cantrip three times, then cast the Cave Fangs buff before switching to a more suitable weapon. The traps will be Empowered and Maximised.

##### Promise of Greatness { #meta-greatness}

??? item "Promise of Greatness"
    {% include '_includes/promise_of_greatness.md' %}

This requires Cave Fangs to be cast in combat as the first spell of that combat. This costs a standard action and prevents equipment changes during combat. A weaker encounter can instead be used to activate the buff, leaving plenty of Bolstered traps available for the boss.

??? item "Duality of Conjuration and Summoning"
    {% include '_includes/duality_of_conjuration_and_summoning.md' %}

This book also offers an opportunity. If the 10% chance of the summoned creatures attacking the player occurs, that encounter can be used to cast the buff.

##### Magus Arcana

??? class-feature "Magus Arcana"
    {% include '_includes/magus_arcana.md' %}

Several Magus Arcana allow metamagic to be applied to the next spell. Activating an Arcana grants a permament buff, which remains active until a suitable spell is cast. Activating the Arcana before Cave Fangs: Stalactites applies the metamagic to the buff and the traps are Empowered and Maximised.

??? class-feature "Empowered Magic"

??? class-feature "Maximized Magic"

Reach Magic is another useful Metamagic Arcana. It has no effect on the buff itself, but applies to the traps without consuming the charge. Provided no other spells are cast, traps can be placed at a greater distance than normal.

It works in much the same way as the Magus Metamagic Arcana.
