Cave Fangs traps are placed as a free action, leaving the standard action available for other abilities. The following options are not exhaustive.

### Dispels

In addition to removing buffs that might increase saving throws or reduce damage, dispels can lower enemy saving throws through the following effects:

- **Dispel Synergy:** A successful dispel applies a –2 penalty to the target's saving throws.
- **Destructive Dispel:** A successful targeted dispel can leave the target stunned or sickened. The sickened condition applies a further –2 penalty to saving throws.

### Hexes { #std-hexes }

Hexes are free standard actions and therefore pair well with Cave Fangs.

Evil Eye reduces enemy saving throws.
