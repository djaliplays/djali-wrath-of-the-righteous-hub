#### Mythic Rank 6 damage potential

The following calculations estimate the damage from a single **Cave Fangs** cast, with Sword of Heaven active and all 144 traps hitting an (infinite hp) target.

Maximised damage is rounded down by the game for each trap. Average damage retains fractional values until the total is calculated.

**Stalactite Trap damage**

These calculations assume Sword of Heaven is active, including the *Bound of Possibility* buff. The Bolster is not applied to the Sword of Heaven extra dice. 

- Base damage with Bolster: \(3d8 + 6\)
- Damage with Sword of Heaven: \(7d8 + 6\)

Plague of Madness provides Empower and Maximise, while Promise of Greatness provides the Bolster effect. Abolish Guile is another 1.5 times multiplier.

\[
\left\lfloor (7 \times 8 + 6) \times 1.5^2  \right\rfloor
= 139
\]

**Theoretical maximum Trap damage**

This setup assumes:

- Hellknight 10 main character (not recommended)
- Level 20 Paladin ally
- Fire Conversion and Flame Curse on the enemy
- A Guile Demon

\[
\left\lfloor
(7 \times 8 + 6 + 20 + 20) \times 1.5^3
\right\rfloor
= 344
\]

**Geomancy damage**

At character level 18 or higher, Geomancy deals:

\[
9d6 \rightarrow 13d6
\]

The average damage with Abolish Guile is:

\[
13 \times \frac{6 + 1}{2} \times 1.5 = 68.25
\]


**Elemental Barrage damage**

Elemental Barrage deals \(6d6\) damage when an enemy is damaged after also having been damaged during the previous turn.

Witch Abolish Guile:

\[
6 \times \frac{6 + 1}{2} \times 1.5 = 31.5
\]

##### Total single-target damage potential

Using all 144 traps against a Flame Cursed enemy, the theoretical damage consists of:

- 344 maximised trap damage per trap
- 68.25 average Geomancy damage per trap

Elemental Barrage is excluded from this total.

**Total across 144 traps:**

\[
(344 + 68.25) \times 144
= \boxed{59\,364 \text{ average damage}}
\]

Wether Elemental Barrage increases this further (you lose Flame Curse multiplier on half the traps) is left as an excercise for the reader.
