### Initiative

The most reliable way to stay alive is to eliminate enemies before they can act. Cave Fangs can kill enemies in a single turn; acting first is therefore essential.

Improved Initiative is the main feat for increasing initiative. Warrior Priest provides a smaller bonus.

#### Main classes { #init-full-class }

Some classes that work with Cave Fangs also provide substantial initiative bonuses at higher levels. Earlier bonuses from dips are covered in [Dips](#init-dips).

**Oracle**

An Oracle can gain Cave Fangs through the Nature Mystery or Loremaster and follow the Angel mythic path. A second mystery, Battle, can be obtained through a mythic ability or a Magic Deceiver: Living Deity dip.

??? class-feature "Warsight"
    {% include '_includes/warsight.md' %}

??? class-feature "Oracle's Curse"
    {% include '_includes/oracles_curse.md' %}

??? class-feature "Powerless Prophecy"
    {% include '_includes/powerless_prophecy.md' %}

The level requirements refer to Oracle levels. A single-level Oracle dip grants the ability to roll twice for initiative through Warsight.

**Geomancer**

A Geomancer does not need to be taken as a dip solely for its bloodline; the class can also support a Lich build, for example. Favored Terrain provides initiative bonuses at later levels.

??? class-feature "Powerless Prophecy"
    {% include '_includes/powerless_prophecy.md' %}

**Inquisitor**

A two-level Inquisitor dip is useful for characters that use Wisdom as their casting or primary ability score.

??? class-feature "Cunning Initiative"
    {% include '_includes/cunning_initiative.md' %}

Cunning Initiative adds Wisdom to initiative checks, while the Luck domain's Bit of Luck can be used to reroll initiative.

The Luck domain requires worship of Shelyn, so a Good starting alignment is necessary.

#### Items

Some items that provide initiative bonuses can be equipped after Cave Fangs has been cast, since changing equipment does not affect the traps once the buff is active. The opportunity cost of most initiative items is low; they can generally be equipped after buffing if available.

Weapons require more investment and are therefore considered separately. Night Flash and Wrath of the Creator both provide initiative bonuses.

??? item "Night Flash"
    {% include '_includes/night_flash.md' %}

??? item "Wrath of the Creator"
    {% include '_includes/wrath_of_the_creator.md' %}

Both weapons require Exotic Weapon Proficiency. Wrath of the Creator is a double weapon, so it cannot be dual-wielded with another weapon, even with Giant Weapon Wielder.
