### Holy Conversion

Several abilities in the game can convert **all** damage dealt by a character to holy damage, or to unholy damage for the Gold Dragon if sworn to serve Dahak.

This includes trap damage, Geomancy damage, and even the direct damage Geomancy deals to the caster. All of it becomes holy or unholy damage.

#### Angel { #conversion-angel }

At mythic rank 6, the Angel gains Sword of Holy Damage, which converts all damage to holy damage while Sword of Heaven is active.

Sword of Heaven can be activated a number of times per day equal to 1 plus half the mythic rank. With Everlasting Flame, its duration increases to 1 minute per mythic rank. At mythic rank 6, this provides four uses lasting 6 minutes each. This is sufficient to support a build centred on holy conversion.

#### Reasons to convert to (un)holy { #conversion-reasons}

- Bypasses the DR or immunity affecting traps and certain Geomancy terrains. Unlike smites, conversion is not limited to a single target and is less restricted by daily uses. See [DR and Incorporeal](#dr-and-incorporeal).
- Bypasses Precision damage immunity, relevant for Surprise Spells.
- Prevents the direct damage from Geomancy with Broken Trickster or Solar Winds. See [Geomancy Backlash](#geomancy-backlash). This is less important with the Gloves of Enduring Wizard.
- Allows further conversion to fire or another element. See [Elemental Conversion](#elemental-conversion).
