#### Geomancy Backlash

Each Geomancy trigger deals 1d8 direct damage to the caster. This becomes problematic when Geomancy is triggered repeatedly through free actions. Direct damage cannot be mitigated by DR or immunity, but several methods can prevent or absorb it.

##### Temporary hit points

This method works with any mythic path.

??? item "Gloves of Enduring Wizard"
    {% include '_includes/gloves_of_enduring_wizard.md' %}

By Act 4, traps count as spells of level 7 or higher. Each trap placement therefore grants 20 temporary hit points. These do not stack, so 144 placements cannot accumulate 144 × (20 - 1d8) hit points. However, the temporary hit points granted by each cast effectively absorb the damage from the following cast.

##### Angel: Solar Winds

The Angel's Sword of Holy Damage converts all damage to holy damage while Sword of Heaven is active. See [Holy Conversion: Angel](#conversion-angel).

Solar Winds grants resistance to holy damage and becomes available at the same mythic rank as the conversion, rank 6.

Sword of Holy Damage is unlocked automatically, whereas Solar Winds requires an Improved Angelic Halo selection.

??? angel-feature "Sword of Holy Damage"
    {% include '_includes/sword_of_holy_damage.md' %}

??? angel-feature "Solar Winds"

At mythic rank 6, Solar Winds provides only 6 resistance against 1d8 damage. Consequently, 1 or 2 damage may still be taken occasionally.

##### Broken Trickster

This method requires all direct damage to be converted to a single energy type. Only a few abilities can convert all damage; see [Holy Conversion](#holy-conversion) and [Negative Conversion](#negative-energy-conversion). Once the damage has been converted, protection against the corresponding energy type can prevent it.

??? item "Broken Trickster"
    {% include '_includes/broken_trickster.md' %}

With this item, the converted damage is taken once. For the remainder of the turn, the wearer is effectively immune to that damage type, as the item grants 20 resistance.

The method works with any energy type, so unholy conversion is just as effective as holy conversion.

##### Death Ward

This is the earliest available solution. The Lich's [Negative Conversion](#negative-energy-conversion), granted by Death of Elements, converts Geomancy's self-damage to negative energy.

Death Ward grants immunity to negative energy, preventing the damage entirely.

!!! Warning "Ascendant Element: Negative"
    Ascendant Element: Negative Energy bypasses the caster's own immunity to negative energy when dealing self-damage.
