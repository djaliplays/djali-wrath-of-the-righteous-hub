## The Ability

Cave Fangs allows traps to be placed across the battlefield. The Stalactites variant grants the ability to place them as a free action. Placing a trap directly on an enemy during combat triggers it immediately.

### Names used in this guide

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Cave Fangs** | The 5th-level conjuration spell, with two variants. |
| **Cave Fangs: Stalactites** | The variant used in this guide. Casting it is a standard action and grants a timed buff. |
| **Stalactites Trap** | The ability granted by the buff, used to place the actual traps. In-game, it is titled "Cave Fangs" with the description: "Create Stalactites trap". |
| **Buff** | Refers to the Cave Fangs: Stalactites buff. |
| **Trap** | Short for Stalactites Trap. |

Both the spell and the traps it creates count as conjuration spells.

### Trap placement 

Placing a trap is a free action, with no fixed limit on the number placed per turn. The practical limit is the buff's remaining duration, as each trap consumes 10 minutes.

The number of available traps is therefore equal to the buff's remaining duration divided by 10 minutes. Since the buff lasts 10 minutes per caster level, this allows one trap per caster level before modifiers. Effects that directly extend the buff's duration provide additional traps (see [Enduring Spells](#enduring-spells)).

Traps remain on the ground for 1 hour per level, although this rarely matters in combat. They can be placed directly on enemies, triggering immediately.

### Trap spell level

The trap's spell level is **half the character level, capped at 9**. It is independent of Cave Fangs' own spell level (5), the spell slot used, and caster level. Several interactions discussed later in this guide depend on the trap's spell level.

### Stalagmites version

The Stalagmites variant uses a swift action to place a trap, limiting placement to one per turn in combat. This is why the guide focuses on Stalactites.
