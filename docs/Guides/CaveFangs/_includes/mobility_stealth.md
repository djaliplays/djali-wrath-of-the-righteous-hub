### Avoiding enemy retaliation

Killing all enemies within range is not always sufficient. In spread-out encounters, or fights with multiple phases, some enemies may remain out of reach. Those enemies can then retaliate on the following turn, particularly with ranged attacks.

Two approaches help prevent this: **mobility** and **stealth**.

#### Mobility

When necessary, both the move action and standard action can be used to reposition. Several spells provide substantial movement bonuses:

An animal companion provides an additional mobility option. It can use both its move action and standard action to move, while its rider retains both actions for combat. This is particularly effective when combined with the movement buffs above.

Classes that have or can gain an animal companion include:

- **Hunter:** 6th-level caster; has Cave Fangs.
- **Sacred Huntmaster:** 6th-level caster.
- **Druid:** 9th-level caster; has Cave Fangs.
- **Oracle with the Nature Mystery:** 9th-level caster; has Cave Fangs.
- **Wildland Shaman:** 9th-level caster; has Cave Fangs.
- **Shaman with the Nature Spirit:** 9th-level caster; has Cave Fangs, but gains the animal companion very late.

#### Stealth

In-combat stealth provides another way to avoid retaliation. Once nearby enemies have been killed, there are no remaining enemies in the area to detect the character. Entering stealth prevents distant enemies from moving in to attack. On the following turn, the character can approach and eliminate them.

Several options provide access to in-combat stealth.

**Spells**

Vanish is a common 1st-level spell available to many classes. By this stage, unused 1st-level spell slots are often readily available.

Only one round of invisibility is required, so Vanish is sufficient; more expensive spells are unnecessary.
