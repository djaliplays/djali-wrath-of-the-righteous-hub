### Demon { #mythic-demon }

#### Mythic Rank 3 { #demon-m3 }

The Demon gains Demonic Rage, initially usable three times per day, with each use lasting for one combat.

For Cave Fangs, Demonic Rage provides +2 DC to both the Cave Fangs buff and the traps themselves. One use can be spent in a minor encounter to cast the buff, retaining the +2 DC bonus for the rest of the dungeon. This leaves two uses available for important encounters, where another +2 DC can be gained, for a total of +4 DC during Rage.

#### Mythic Rank 4 { #demon-m4 }

The Demon gains a minor Demon Aspect.

**Brimorak** appears to offer additional damage, but its bonus only applies to Fire spells and therefore does not benefit Stalactite Traps.

??? demon-feature "Aspect of Brimorak"

**Incubus** provides another +1 DC during Demonic Rage.

    {% include '_includes/aspect_incubus.md' %}

**Babau**, the alternative, provides additional sneak attack damage, which benefits Surprise Spells.

#### Mythic Rank 5 {demon-m5}

At Mythic Rank 5, the Demon gains another minor Aspect and two major Aspects. Two minor Aspects can now be active simultaneously.

??? demon-feature "Extra Minor Aspect"
    {% include '_includes/demon_extra_minor_aspect.md' %}

**Vrock** imposes a –1 penalty to enemy Reflex saves. 

??? demon-feature "Aspect of Vrock"
    {% include '_includes/aspect_vrock.md' %}

Two major Aspects provide bonuses to the casting ability score, and therefore to DC:

- **Shadow Demon** increases Wisdom.
- **Coloxus** increases Intelligence.

These ability score bonuses are passive.

??? demon-feature "Aspect of Coloxus"

At this stage, the additional benefit is +1 DC (or +2 DC with uneven casting ability score).

No other major Aspects provide relevant synergy with Cave Fangs.

#### Mythic Rank 6 { #demon-m6 }

Demonic Rage increases to four uses per day. Most Aspect benefits also improve.

The additional minor Aspects provide no further benefits relevant to Cave Fangs.

Incubus is always used for the buffing encounter. 

For major encounters, the choice is between Incubus and Vrock for higher DC, or Babau and Vrock for additional sneak attack damage if using Suprise Spells.

| Configuration | Buff DC | Effective Trap DC | Suprise Spells |
|---|---:|---:| ---- :|
| Incubus + Any -> Incubus + Vrock | +7 | +14 | |
| Incubus + Any -> Babau + Vrock | +7 | +12 | +3d6|

#### Mythic Rank 9 { #demon-m9 }

Demonic Rage increases to five uses per day. Most Aspect benefits improve again.

| Configuration | Buff DC | Effective Trap DC | Suprise Spells |
|---|---:|---:| ---- :|
| Incubus + Any -> Incubus + Vrock | +9 | +19 | |
| Incubus + Any -> Babau + Vrock | +9 | +16 | +4d6|

The Demon also gains access to a Demon Lord Aspect.
{: #demon-deskari }

**Deskari** allows the Demon to bypass physical damage immunity.

#### Mythic Rank 10

+1 DC from Coloxus or Shadow Demon.


#### Discussion

The Demon offers the highest trap DC of the mythic paths discussed here, alongside additional sneak attack damage for Surprise Spells builds.

The main limitation is the lack of damage conversion. Deskari provides a way to bypass physical damage immunity, but only at Mythic Rank 9.
