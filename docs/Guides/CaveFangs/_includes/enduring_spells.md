### Enduring Spells

??? mythic-feature "Enduring Spells"
    {% include '_includes/enduring_spells.md' %}

Enduring Spells extends the Cave Fangs buff to 24 hours. Since each trap consumes 10 minutes of the buff, this allows **144 traps from a single cast**. 

#### Timing 

The first mythic ability becomes available at the end of Act 1. Solo characters gain more XP, so reaching level 10 by this point is only possible when playing solo (see the [Shared Experience Addendum](../SharedXP/wotr_graphs.md)). Soloing until Gray Garrison, without necessarily soloing the location itself, allows level 9 to be reached before Act 2. Either way, Enduring Spells and Cave Fangs can work together almost immediately.

The drawback is that physical DR remains difficult to overcome, while a single trap deals little damage. Enemies with physical DR therefore require many traps to kill. See [DR and Incorporeal](#dr-and-incorporeal).

Mythic Rank 3, reached at the end of Act 2, is another good opportunity to take it. In Act 3, trap damage can be increased through [Plague of Madness](#meta-plague) and the [mythic choice](#mythic-angel). A Paladin can also provide [Mark of Justice](#mark-of-justice) to help overcome DR on bosses.
