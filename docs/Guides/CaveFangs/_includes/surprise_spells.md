### Surprise Spells

??? class-feature "Surprise Spells"
    {% include '_includes/surprise_spells.md' %}

Surprise Spells adds sneak attack damage to all your traps, which is a very significant damage boost.

There are some limits:

- The damage is of the same type as the spell, but it is still sneak (precision) damage. Enemies that are immune to precision damage are immune to it. Can be bypassed by [holy conversion](#holy-conversion) for Incorporeal Enemies and with [negative energy conversion](#negative-energy-conversion) *with* Ascendant Element: Negative for all.
- The damage is not maximized or empowered along with the traps.
- With double conversion, for example holy or negative energy converted on to fire, the sneak damage stays as the first type, while the damage of the traps is converted onwards to fire.
- Geomancy does not get the sneak attack damage.
- The usual sneak attack synergy (such as Petrifying Strike) require an attack roll to work. 

#### Uncanny Dodge

Enemies with Uncanny Dodge cannot be caught flat-footed. The Arcane Trickster's Impromptu Sneak Attack helps with this, as the target loses its Dexterity bonus to AC against the attacks.

Alter Ego will do the same but much more economically, but requires three level of Assassin.

#### Arcane Trickster 

Surprise Spells comes from the Arcane Trickster prestige class. The prerequisites are:

| Requirement | |
|---|---|
| Skills | Mobility 4, Trickery 4, Knowledge (Arcana) 4 |
| Sneak attack | 2d6 |
| Spells | Arcane spells of 2nd level |

The recommended route is 1 level of Rowdy, then 3 levels of Wizard or Witch (taking the Accomplished Sneak Attacker feat at character level 3), then 10 levels of Arcane Trickster.
