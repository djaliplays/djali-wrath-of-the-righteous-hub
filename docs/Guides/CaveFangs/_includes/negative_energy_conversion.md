### Negative Energy Conversion

The Lich mythic path provides an ability that converts all damage dealt to negative energy.

#### Death of Elements

??? mythic-feature "Death of Elements"
    {% include '_includes/death_of_elements.md' %}

Although the description specifies elemental damage, the ability appears to convert all damage. As with holy conversion, this includes both Geomancy's direct self-damage and its area damage.

The ability becomes available as early as mythic rank 3, considerably earlier than the Angel's equivalent at rank 6.

Since Death Ward grants immunity to negative energy, the Geomancy combination can be used as soon as mythic rank 3. Alternatively Dhampir's have Negative Energy Affinity.

#### Downsides compared to (un)holy { #neg-downsides }

- Some enemies, notably undead, are immune to negative energy.
- Damage against incorporeal enemies is still halved, whereas holy damage deals full damage. See [DR and Incorporeal](#dr-and-incorporeal).
- Does not bypass Precision Immunity to Surprise Spells sneak damage.

The first issue can be addressed with Ascendant Element: Negative Energy or the Vulnerability Curse hex. See [Resistances and immunities](#resistances-and-immunities).

The last only by Ascendant Element: Negative Energy. 

!!! Warning "Ascendant Element: Negative Energy"
    Ascendant Element: Negative Energy bypasses the caster's own immunity to negative energy, leaving the caster vulnerable to Geomancy self-damage. It is therefore inadvisable before obtaining the [Gloves of Enduring Wizard](#temporary-hit-points).

The following item provides a solution to the first two issues, but only for trap damage.

??? item "Devouring Lust"
    {% include '_includes/devouring_lust.md' %}

The Maximise effect does not work with Cave Fangs, as intended. However, the item also converts negative energy damage to unholy damage without consuming its charges, making this conversion effectively free.

??? note "Conversion from Holy"
    The item also works with damage converted to holy in the previous section, although there is no practical reason to use it for that purpose.

This conversion affects only trap damage, not Geomancy's direct self-damage or area damage. Nor does it affect the Sneak damage from Surprise Spells.

Since Ascendant Element: Negative Energy is inadvisable because of the self-damage, Death of Elements can be toggled off when fighting groups of undead. Vulnerability Curse can then be used against undead bosses with high DR.
