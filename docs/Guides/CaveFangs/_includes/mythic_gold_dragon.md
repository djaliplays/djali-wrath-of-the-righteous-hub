### Gold Dragon { #mythic-gd }

The Gold Dragon loses all mythic features belonging to the previous path.

The exception is a merged spellbook (Angel and Lich). An unmerged mythic spellbook is lost.

#### Mythic 10 { #gd-m10 }

With Perfect Soul, damage dice become d10s. This applies to both the regular trap damage and Surprise Spells. In addition, spells (and abilities) are automatically Empowered and Maximised, this includes the traps (but not the sneak attack), Geomancy and even [Call of the Fiery Things](#fire-items) procs.

For traps, this removes the need to use Plague of Madness. The greater benefit is to Geomancy, which is also Empowered and Maximised. A standard Geomancy proc now deals:

9 × 6 × 1.5 = 81 damage

The Empower effect also applies to additional flat damage riders on Geomancy, such as Dragon Smite or an allied Paladin's Mark of Justice.

Some recommendations:

Grand Cognatogen provides a +8 bonus to one mental ability score, +6 to another and +4 to a third. It is particularly useful with Dragon Smite. If the casting ability is not Charisma, Charisma can be selected as the second ability score for a +6 bonus.

??? class-feature "Grand Cognatogen"
    {% include '_includes/grand_cognatogen.md' %}

At least one level of Alchemist or Mutation Warrior is required to use the ability. Vivisectionist may also provide the prerequisite Sneak Dice for the Arcane Trickster prestige class (link to be added), making it a potential choice for builds focused on Surprise Spells.

Extra Magus Arcana: Reach Magic is a useful for extending trap placement range, particularly against spread-out enemies.

??? class-feature "Reach Magic"
    {% include '_includes/magus_arcana_reach_magic.md' %}

Flame Curse is not recommended. Dragon Breath already provides a strong standard-action option.

Flame Curse is only better in a theoretical scenario with a single enemy with a higher HP value then any enemy in the game has. 

Otherwise Flame Curse requires a substantial amount of additional flat damage on traps to be worthwhile compared with Dragon Breath. This means having Hellknight levels, an allied Paladin's Mark of Justice and Dragon Smite.

However quickening Dragon Smite through Boots of Arcane Persistence conflicts with Dragon Might, as both spells require a swift action.

Flame Curse can also be cast by an ally.

The Gold Dragon also gains two further Dragon Breath upgrades.

#### Discussion

The Gold Dragon has the highest damage ceiling for a Cave Fangs build.

Perfect Soul also changes the sneak attack dice from Surprise Spells to d10s, making the Gold Dragon particularly effective for an Arcane Trickster build.

It is arguably the strongest continuation for an Arcane Trickster Lich build, as the Lich offers fewer options for increasing Cave Fangs damage at higher Mythic Ranks.

The Angel already has very high damage potential, so switching to the Gold Dragon offers less of an advantage.

Dragon Smite is not essential to the build and competes with Dragon Might for spell slots. This also means that the build does not need to be Charisma-based.
