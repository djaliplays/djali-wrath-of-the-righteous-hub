### Smites

A smite adds flat damage to the damage rolls of the character using it and bypasses the target's DR. This makes smites a useful, although resource-limited, means of overcoming physical DR. The bonus damage also applies to Geomancy, creating a synergy between the two abilities.

#### The smites

Several smites are available. Apart from the Paladin's Smite Evil, they differ in damage per class level, the level at which they become available, and the number of daily uses.

| Smite | Damage per level | Gained at | Uses per day |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Smite Evil** (Paladin) | 1 | 1 | 1, +1 at level 4 and every 3 levels after (7 at level 19) |
| **Smite Chaos** (Hellknight) | 2 | 1 | 1, +1 at level 4 and every 3 levels after (4 at level 10) |
| **All Is Darkness** (Tortured Crusader) | 1 | 1 | 1, +1 every 5 levels (5 at level 20, not counting 3 more from Unbroken Paragon) |
| **Sworn Enemy - Smite** (Faith Hunter) | 0.5 | 6 | 1, +1 at level 10 and every 4 levels after (4 at level 18) |
| **Smite** (Champion of the Faith) | 1 | 4 | 1, +1 every 4 levels beyond 4th (5 at level 20); consumes 2 uses of Fervor |

Two aspects of the descriptions require clarification. The phrase "all damage rolls" refers to damage rolls made by the character who activated the smite. The DR bypass also applies to damage dealt by that character's spells.

#### Synergy with the traps and Geomancy

- Smite bypasses DR for the traps, and for Geomancy when it deals physical damage (Desert, Underground and Urban). See [DR and Incorporeal](#dr-and-incorporeal).
- The bonus damage applies to both the traps and Geomancy.
- When added to trap damage, the bonus is maximised and empowered.
- Other spell damage multipliers also apply to the smite bonus.

#### Class considerations

The Hellknight is the most level-efficient source of additional damage, making further investment in the class the stronger option for damage scaling.

A single Paladin level can instead provide DR bypass. Combined with the scroll and the ring, this grants three daily uses of Smite Evil: one from the first Paladin level and one from each item.

#### Mark of Justice

The Paladin also gains Mark of Justice, which grants all allies the ability to smite evil for 1 minute, using the Paladin's bonuses. An allied Paladin can therefore provide these bonuses without requiring any Paladin levels in the main build.
